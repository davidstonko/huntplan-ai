## Description (4000 char max)
```
MDHuntFishOutdoors brings all of Maryland's scattered DNR information together in one free, offline-first app — built for Maryland hunters, anglers, campers, and hikers.

No account. No ads. Works without cell service in the field.

— FOUR MODES, ONE APP —
Switch between Hunt, Fish, Camp, and Hike. Each gives you the maps, regulations, and tools for that activity.

— HUNT —
• Full map of Maryland's 192 public hunting lands (WMAs, state forests, CFLs, NRMAs) plus shooting ranges, with filters by species, weapon, and access.
• Property lines: see parcel boundaries with owner mailing info, and which land is likely public (huntable) vs private — tap any parcel to look up the owner on the official Maryland SDAT record.
• "Can I Hunt?" checker: pick a species, weapon, date, and county to see if you're in season, plus today's legal shooting hours.
• Seasons, bag limits, deer regions, Sunday-hunting rules, blaze-orange and tagging requirements.
• Scout tab: drop waypoints, record GPS tracks, measure distances, plan your hunt, and read wind/scent for stand placement.
• Deer Camp: shared maps with your hunting group.

— FISH —
• 737 DNR public angler-access sites, boat ramps, tide stations, and trout-stocking waters.
• Live USGS stream gauges: real-time flow, gauge height, and water temperature.
• Wild trout waters shaded by species (native brook, wild brown, wild rainbow) from DNR survey data.
• Tides, stocking reports, species info, and regulations.

— CAMP & HIKE —
• Maryland campgrounds, state parks, and the Appalachian Trail (shelters, trailheads, landmarks, Four States Challenge).
• Trip planners that build a packing list for your trip.

— OFFLINE MAPS —
Download the exact area you're heading into so the map works deep in the woods with zero signal.

— AI GUIDE —
Ask plain-English questions about regulations, seasons, licenses, and where to go — answered from a built-in Maryland knowledge base, no connection required.

— GEAR —
Curated Maryland-tested gear picks (Amazon Associate links).

MDHuntFishOutdoors is a planning tool, not legal advice. Always verify current rules with the Maryland Department of Natural Resources, the U.S. Forest Service, the National Park Service, and the Appalachian Trail Conservancy before you head out. Not affiliated with Maryland DNR.
```
