================================================================================
KEY DISCOVERIES & INSIGHTS
================================================================================

HIGH-VALUE DATA FOR APP:

1. CROSSBOW HUNTING SURGE
   - Now 60% of archery harvest (was minority 5 years ago)
   - Permits required but enable year-round deer hunting
   - Should be prominently featured in seasonal guides

2. DEAL ISLAND & FAIRMOUNT IMPOUNDMENT HUNTS
   - Permit-based waterfowl hunts with fixed open seasons
   - Deal Island: Sept 1-15 & Nov 1-Feb 7, 2025-26
   - Require formal application (copy of license + migratory bird stamp)
   - Could be calendar alerts in app

3. CWD MANAGEMENT AREA RESTRICTIONS
   - 7 western MD counties have transport restrictions
   - Whole carcasses cannot leave zone (deboned meat OK with confirmation)
   - Critical for hunter safety/compliance education

4. LOTTERY OPPORTUNITIES
   - Bear hunt lottery (July 12-Aug 31, $15 fee)
   - Chesapeake Forest tract lottery (Aug 21 drawing)
   - Mentored deer hunts (Sept 1+ application)
   - Could trigger calendar reminders in app

5. COMMUNITY INSIGHTS
   - Forum data shows regional success variations
   - Suburban MD has highest deer density & quality
   - Western MD has more pressure relief despite less quality
   - Sunday hunting popular (9,459 harvest) in 7 counties

6. YOUTH PARTICIPATION GROWTH
   - Junior hunters up 12% YoY (2,493 in 2024-25)
   - Free annual licenses available upon safety course completion
   - App should feature youth programs prominently
