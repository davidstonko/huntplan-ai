# Maryland Hunting Knowledge Base - Comprehensive Research

This directory contains deeply researched Maryland hunting data compiled from official DNR sources, community forums, and regulatory documents.

## Files Overview

### 1. special_hunts.json
Managed hunts, lottery draws, and special opportunities including:
- Chesapeake Forest Hunting Tract Lottery (hunt club leases)
- Mentored Deer Hunts (lottery-based)
- Stocked Pheasant Hunts
- Deal Island & Fairmount WMA Impoundment Hunts (permit-based)
- Free Public Hunting Permit Program
- Military base hunting opportunities (requires base-specific inquiry)

**Key Insight:** Deal Island and Fairmount WMA have formal impoundment hunting programs with application deadlines and dates. These are premium waterfowl hunting opportunities.

### 2. deer_management.json
Deer management information:
- **CWD Management Area (CWDMA)** - 7 western counties with CWD surveillance
  - Carcass transport restrictions (whole deer cannot leave zone)
  - Deboned meat transport allowed with check-in confirmation
- **Deer Management Permits (DMP)** - For landowner crop damage (antlerless only)
- **Antler Point Restrictions** - Statewide 3-point minimum for one buck
- **Sika Deer Hunting** - Eastern Shore specialty hunt (requires $10 stamp)
- **2024-2025 Harvest Data:**
  - 84,201 total deer (32,148 antlered, 47,271 antlerless)
  - Archery increased 16% YoY
  - Crossbows now 60% of archery harvest
  - Junior hunters took 2,493 (12% increase)

**Key Insight:** Earn-A-Buck program exists but county-specific designations vary—check current guide for details.

### 3. license_fees.json
Complete license and stamp pricing for 2025-2026:
- **Resident Regular:** $35 (ages 16-64)
- **Senior (65+):** $5
- **Junior (under 16):** $10.50 (includes archery + muzzleloader stamps)
- **Apprentice:** $15 (resident), $40 (nonresident) - one-time only, requires safety course
- **Nonresident Regular:** $160
- **Disabled Veterans:** Free lifetime license (requires VA certification)
- **Stamps:** Archery $6, Muzzleloader $6, Sika Deer $10, Bonus Buck $10, Migratory Bird $9, Federal Duck $25
- **HIP Registration:** Included with Migratory Bird Stamp ($9)

**Key Insight:** Lifetime licenses available at DNR Service Centers but pricing not publicly listed—varies by county.

### 4. waterfowl_data.json
Waterfowl hunting opportunities:
- **Blind Lottery & Reservations** - Phone-based reservation system (max 4 days advance)
- **Sea Duck Zone** - Coastal scoters, eiders, mergansers
- **Impoundment Hunts** - Deal Island WMA Sept 1-15 & Nov 1-Feb 7
- **Required Licenses:**
  - Maryland Migratory Game Bird Stamp ($9 + HIP registration)
  - Federal Duck Stamp ($25)
- **Bag Limits:** Species and zone-specific (see official guide)

**Key Insight:** Non-toxic shot required. Blind access often requires calling 4 days in advance or lottery for first Saturday.

### 5. bear_hunting.json
Black bear hunting:
- **Permit:** Lottery-based allocation ($15 application fee)
- **Zones:** Zone 1 (Allegany, Frederick, Garrett, Washington) and Zone 2 (Frederick, Washington)
- **Bag Limit:** 1 bear per hunting team per season
- **Lottery Period:** July 12 - August 31 (check current year)
- **Methods:** Firearms or archery allowed

**Key Insight:** Western Maryland only. Population naturally recolonizing from PA/WV. Competition for permits high.

### 6. community_tips.json
Forum-sourced hunting strategies from ArcheryTalk, Bowhunting.com, and other communities:

**Regional Insights:**
- **Western MD:** Tons of public land, minimal pressure, but smaller/fewer bucks
- **Suburban MD:** More deer/sq mi, higher quality bucks, more pressure, need to scout
- **Eastern Shore:** Sika deer specialty, coastal marsh habitat

**Tactical Tips:**
- Tree stand placement along deer corridors (check wind direction)
- Early archery hunting on WMAs often productive
- Scout to find feeding/roosting areas
- Upland birds (pheasant, quail) favor specific cover types
- Waterfowl success via decoys and natural shoreline concealment

**WMA Highlights:**
- **Fishing Bay WMA** - 29,000 acres, largest tidal wetland public land, white-tailed + sika deer
- **Taylor's Island WMA** - 1,120 acres, marsh/forest mix
- **Browns Branch WMA** - Hard-packed trails, can be muddy, no Sunday hunting

### 7. harvest_data.json
Historical and recent harvest statistics:
- **2024-2025 Total:** 84,201 deer (most recent season)
- **Archery Breakdown:** 28,775 total (39% vertical, 61% crossbow)
- **Sunday Hunting:** 9,459 deer in permitted counties (Allegany, Cecil, Garrett, St. Mary's, Washington, Wicomico, Worcester)
- **Junior Hunters:** 2,493 (12% YoY increase) - encourages youth participation
- **County-by-County Data:** Available in full Big Game Reports

**Key Insight:** Crossbow growth is dramatic—now 60% of archery harvest. Youth participation trending up.
