================================================================================
MOST IMPORTANT DATA FOR USERS
================================================================================

1. WHEN CAN I FISH?
   - Striped bass: April (C&R only), May-Sept (harvest), skip August
   - Trout: Feb-Mar stocking season (put-and-take)
   - Other species: Year-round (unless noted)

2. HOW MUCH CAN I KEEP?
   - Bass: 5 fish (all types)
   - Trout: 2 combined (brook+brown) or 5 (other types)
   - Walleye: 5 fish (watch Deep Creek Lake slot)
   - Striped bass: 1 fish (19-24" in Bay, 28-31" ocean)

3. DO I NEED A LICENSE?
   - Yes: $32/year (resident nontidal) or $55 (nonresident)
   - No: Under 16 years old
   - Maybe: Trout stamp ($20) if targeting trout

4. WHERE IS IT SAFE TO EAT?
   - Check MDE interactive map for consumption advisories
   - Be careful: Mercury, PCBs, and PFAS in some waters

5. STOCKING DATES
   - Call 800-688-3467 for current schedule
   - Updated Fridays during stocking season
   - Check stocking_schedule.json for initial times
